## It is built like the Warehouse master, not like a scene plate

Empty room, nobody in it, wide enough to hold the whole floor in one view. Section 6.1 made
that call for the Warehouse because eleven images happen there and two independently-generated
references would have been each internally consistent and clearly not the same building. The
gym only carries two spreads, but it has a harder problem than the Warehouse does.

## The one thing this reference exists to fix is a sightline

*"The bag came off the chain, flew eleven feet and took out the water cooler."*

**That sentence is geometry, and a plate can contradict it.** If the reference hangs the bag
in a corner, or stands the cooler behind the desk, or puts a bench between them, then the
funniest beat in the section stops being possible in the room the reader has just been shown.
So the prompt places them on the same clear run of floor, eleven feet apart, with nothing in
between — and says so twice, once in the description and once as a MUST HOLD.

**The room is generated in its BEFORE state**: bag hanging, cooler upright, bottle full. The
after is a scene plate. That is the same state-change discipline as `MAPLE_AND_FOURTH` A and
B, and it only works because the before exists as a reference first.

## What the prose fixes, all of it

| Feature | Where the prose establishes it |
|---|---|
| A glass front door | *"the door is glass and the sign says OPEN"* |
| A desk just inside it, in view from the street | *"there's a man sitting right there doing paperwork"* — which is why nobody has ever knocked |
| A mat inside the door, filthy | *"a mat that hadn't been swept since March"* |
| A heavy bag on a chain | *"Can I use the heavy bag?"* / *"came off the chain"* |
| A water cooler, eleven feet away, in its path | *"flew eleven feet and took out the water cooler"* |
| Open at five thirty, and this is six in the morning | *"opening that gym at five thirty for twenty-six years"* |
| Twenty-six years of wear | as above |

## The OPEN sign is prose only, and must not be drawn

The negative block forbids signage copy, so the sign hangs in the door as a blank card and
the word lives in the sentence. Same arrangement as Rocco's GOLDEN GLOVES, Wren's stencil,
and every other piece of lettering in the series.

## Two things deliberately excluded

- **A boxing ring.** A ring would fill the floor the bag has to fly across, and nothing in the
  prose needs one. It is excluded by name so the generator does not helpfully add it.
- **Anyone in the room**, including Benny at his own desk. A reference with a figure in it
  stops being a reference — the same rule that kept Ida's wave out of her sheet.
