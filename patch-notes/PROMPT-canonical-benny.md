## What the manuscript fixes, and it is almost nothing

He has no physical description at all. Four facts, and one of them is a constraint:

- **He has been opening that gym at five thirty for twenty-six years.** That is the only
  hard number attached to him, and it puts a floor under his age — you cannot run a gym for
  twenty-six years and be thirty. **So the supplied art is kept and aged up into the mid
  fifties.** The platinum spikes and shaved sides survive; they just belong to a man who has
  been bleaching them for a while. If you would rather he were young, the fix is in the prose
  and it is one number.
- **He does paperwork at a desk by the glass door**, which is why nobody has ever knocked —
  *"the door is glass and the sign says OPEN and there's a man sitting right there doing
  paperwork."* The reading glasses up in his hair are that, carried onto the sheet.
- **He let Rocco use the bag because he wanted to see what would happen.** That is the whole
  character: amused, unhurried, unbothered by a stranger in a robe. The wry heavy-lidded look
  in your art is exactly right and is the thing to protect.
- **He is the one who phones Milo** — *"Milo, he asked me for permission to drink water."* So
  he is the arc's first reaction shot, the ordinary adult who tells you how strange this is.

## The one real problem: he and Rocco share a scene

**Benny is in a white hoodie and Rocco is in a dark grey one, and they are alone together in
an empty gym for two spreads.** That scene is Rocco's introduction, so it is the worst
possible place for two men in hoodies to blur. Four separations, all deliberate:

| | Benny | Rocco |
|---|---|---|
| Hair | platinum spikes, shaved sides | **bald** |
| Hoodie | **white, zip-front, worn open** over a tee | dark grey, pullover |
| Hands | empty | **grey leather boxing gloves, always** |
| Expression | wry, heavy-lidded, ahead of you | apprehensive, eyes up and away |
| Age | mid fifties | twenty-five |

The gloves do most of the work — Rocco is never without them. **Do not frame the two of them
alike**, and in any plate where both appear, Benny should be the one at rest.

## Two smaller collisions, noted and not redesigned

- **Ida also has white hair.** Hers is silver and aged, in close natural curls on a woman in
  her seventies; his is bleached platinum in shaved-side spikes. They never share a scene.
- **The hoodie carries no lettering**, the same arrangement as Rocco's GOLDEN GLOVES and
  Wren's stencil: the negative block forbids text in an image, so it lives in the prose.

## His gym is a separate job
