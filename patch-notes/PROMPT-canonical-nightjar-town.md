**Rosa must be neutral on the sheet.** The morning she closes the truck she is sitting on her
own bumper with her keys in her hand and the CLOSED sign written and not taped up — stillness,
not tears. That belongs to the plate, the same rule that kept Ida's wave out of hers.

**Mr. Jennings has been in two arcs already with no reference**, which is exactly how a
character drifts. He asks the Mayor *“inspecting what, exactly?”* in The Understudies and here
he opens the store at two in the morning in his slippers. One man, one performance: helpful
past the point of usefulness. The slippers belong to the plate.

**The bear is the last image of the arc and the joke depends on it being ordinary.** Everything
funny in *“sitting in the crosswalk outside Carter's Hardware at nine in the morning, enormous
and calm and entirely real, while Mayor Dalton walked toward it with his phone out”* is carried
by the people. The bear does nothing. It is emphatically not one of the Warehouse animals.
