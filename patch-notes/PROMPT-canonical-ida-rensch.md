## [ ] Ida Prewitt

Save as: **`char-ida-canonical.png`** · aspect 3:4

Invented within the project's style, because the manuscript gives her two sentences and no description at all: she walks her beagles at six in the morning, and she waves at the trucks. Everything below is a choice, and every choice is steering AWAY from something already taken.

She is deliberately not wearing a cap, because Wren has a knitted eared cap and Reyes a navy ball cap in the same arc. Her hair is not teal, because Wren's and Vex's are. She has no headscarf, because that is Aunt Carol's one identifying feature, and no reading glasses on a chain and no green cardigan, because the woman in the green cardigan owns both. A buttercup-yellow anorak is worn by nobody in either arc.

The beagles are in her sheet rather than in a separate animal reference. She never appears without them, they are half the joke, and one generation covering two subjects beats two generations.

Her wave is NOT in this sheet, and that is deliberate. The other eighteen canonical references are all neutral pose, doing nothing, and a sheet that performs an action stops being a reference. The wave belongs in the plate.

## [ ] Bill Rensch, the county administrator

Save as: **`char-rensch-canonical.png`** · aspect 3:4

Described from the supplied art. Two things the manuscript already fixes and a plate should be able to carry: the curfew belongs to him -- "Crane is going to want a curfew he can actually enforce, and he can't have one, because the curfew belongs to the dumbest man in this county" -- and he is the Mayor's rival for ceremonial glory. Dalton's nightmare is "Bill Rensch would be standing at my ribbon, in front of my banner, holding my scissors, and I would be down at the other end of it like a boy at a wedding." He also owns a pontoon boat, which Dalton says the way other people say something unforgivable.

He is the FOURTH bald man in the series, after Owen, Mayor Dalton and Rocco, and Rocco is the closest: both bald with beards. They separate on skin tone, spectacles, age and dress -- Rensch is pale, bespectacled, sixty and in an overcoat and waistcoat; Rocco is brown-skinned, unbespectacled, twenty-five and in a hoodie -- and they never share a scene. Noted rather than redesigned, but do not frame them alike.
