Everything the block asks for is present: the white step-van with its hatch propped up on a strut, the striped awning, the fryer and the warmer visible inside, the folding table with the condiment tray, the small red generator on the gravel at the rear with its cable running in, and the backstop and bleachers of the little league field behind.
