# The old school, state C

Use `loc-old-school-c.png` as the required location reference for `{{LOC:OLD_SCHOOL_C}}`. Preserve the layout, the palette and the paper construction. Locked 2026-08-07; see section 6 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md`. Two pages.

The third state of a place the series has now drawn three times. `OLD_SCHOOL` is the building abandoned after the Great Event. `OLD_SCHOOL_B` is the same six acres occupied as a military camp through The Understudies. This is what it looks like after the unit pulled out in May.

**The emptiness is the picture, and the two details that carry it both arrived.**

The FLATTENED RECTANGLES where the tents stood are clearly visible in the dead grass as paler patches of crushed growth. They are the only evidence left in this arc that any of the previous one happened, and they are the entire reason this is a third generation rather than a re-use of `OLD_SCHOOL`.

The PLAY STRUCTURE has its swings removed, leaving the crossbar and the cut chains hanging. That detail has been in the roster since arc one and this is the first time it has been drawn.

Also correct: the nine-foot fence still standing, taut and straight, with its green privacy slats; the vehicle gate shut and padlocked; the ground-floor windows boarded with plywood; six acres of dead grass; and nothing at all inside the fence — no trucks, no tents, no generator, no equipment.

**The Nightjar points at this building from a water tower catwalk** and says something has been under it for six months and nobody has ever made it explain itself. Later a directional dish on the feed store roof is aimed at it. Neither is in this reference, which is the `loc-square` rule: a plate that needs them puts them there.
