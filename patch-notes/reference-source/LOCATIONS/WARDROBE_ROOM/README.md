# The wardrobe room

Use `WARDROBE_ROOM_CANONICAL.png` as the required location reference wherever this room appears. Take the room from it and nothing else: geometry, fittings, wear and paper construction. Camera angle, staging, figures and time of day belong to the individual plate.

`{{LOC:WARDROBE_ROOM}}`. Locked 2026-07-31; see section 6 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md`.

**This is state A.** Trunk seven open with its folded tissue paper, and the small door SHUT. Volume 4 is state B, with that door standing open — and beyond it a second room this reference does not show at all.

**The small door is the whole reason this reference exists.** It is a door, not a hatch: it stands at floor level and a short person walks through it. Attempt 1 drew it as a panel high in the wall, which would have made nonsense of Chet stepping through and pulling it shut. Never raise it.

**The trunk row runs off frame to the left.** Twelve read in view; the prose says thirteen and the room continues around the near corner. Do not treat the visible count as the canon count.

**No legible text anywhere.** The inventory sheet in its sleeve is unreadable and the trunk numbers are prose only — the negative block forbids numerals. Trunk seven identifies itself by being the only open one.
