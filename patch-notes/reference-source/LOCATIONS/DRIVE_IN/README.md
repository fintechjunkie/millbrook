## Two recorded deviations that a plate has to handle

**There is NO external stair up to the projection booth**, and the block asked for one. Junie "came out of the booth with a flashlight when she heard them on the gravel", so she has to be able to get down. Any plate that moves her between the booth and the lot must put a stair in explicitly — this reference is authoritative about its absence, which is the `loc-square` rule.

**The booth's windows are wide and glazed here**, where `loc-projection-booth.png` shows a small square projection port from inside. The two references disagree slightly. The interior one governs the port; this one governs the silhouette.

Also: sunset rather than the pre-dawn the prompt asked for, and a board fence rather than chain link. Neither matters — the story's dawn light is a plate decision.
