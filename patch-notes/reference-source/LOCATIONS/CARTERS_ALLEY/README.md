# Behind Carter's Hardware

Use `loc-carters-alley.png` as the required location reference for `{{LOC:CARTERS_ALLEY}}`. Preserve the layout, the palette and the paper construction. Locked 2026-08-07; see section 6 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md`.

**This is where he lands badly.** He goes off the back of the roof "in a way that involved a rope and a bicycle frame, and the sound he made landing was not a good sound", and eleven seconds later there is a thumbs up over the parapet. So the rear elevation needs a fire escape and a yard to land in, and both are here.

The iron fire escape runs down the rear of the three-storey brick building from the top floor to a drop ladder above the ground door. Bins, crates and pallets against the walls, a board fence on one side, a pole and lines overhead.

**Not to be confused with `{{LOC:FEED_STORE_ALLEY}}`**, which is a different alley behind a different building, where Chet and Pip sit on a milk crate and a paint bucket for twenty minutes. That one is still outstanding.
