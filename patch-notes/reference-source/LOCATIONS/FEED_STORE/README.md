# The feed store: roof and alley

Use `loc-feed-store.png` as the required location reference for `{{LOC:FEED_STORE_ROOF}}` and `{{LOC:FEED_STORE_ALLEY}}`. Preserve the layout, the palette and the paper construction. Locked 2026-08-07 on the SECOND attempt; see section 6 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md`. Five pages.

One image holds both places on purpose, because a plate needs to know how they sit together: the roof he keeps a listening post on for nine nights, and the alley directly below it where Chet reads Felix out of Pip's memory.

**Everything load-bearing arrived.** The cast-iron drainpipe runs from the ground up and OVER the parapet onto the roof, which is the route he climbs. The roof is flat, gravelled, bare and swept, with a low parapet and a brick chimney. The alley below has the plain steel back door, the dumpster, the pallets, and — the detail this reference exists for — a plastic MILK CRATE and an upturned metal PAINT BUCKET standing about a foot apart. Chet and Pip sit on those two for twenty minutes while Vex watches.

**The brick is purple**, matching the Warehouse.

**Nothing the story adds later is present**, which is the `loc-square` rule: no directional dish, no recorder, no cable, no rope and no anchor. Any plate that needs the listening post has to put it there explicitly.

## How this one was generated, because it is worth knowing

The first attempt came back photographic and was rejected. The second run, with the style requirement hoisted to the head of the prompt, produced FIVE variants of this same image and never ran the second prompt in the file at all — so the old school had to be asked for separately, in a file containing one prompt and nothing else.

Four variants are kept for comparison under `patch-notes/incoming/variants-feed-store/`. This one was chosen because its drainpipe visibly continues over the parapet onto the roof, so the climb reads in a single picture, and because the crate and the bucket are the clearest of the five. `feed-store-v2-alternate.png` is the runner-up and is brighter overall.
