# The feed store: roof and alley

Use `loc-feed-store.png` as the required location reference for `{{LOC:FEED_STORE_ROOF}}` and `{{LOC:FEED_STORE_ALLEY}}`. Preserve the layout, the palette and the paper construction. Locked 2026-08-07 from the sixth generation; see section 6 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md`. Five pages.

One image holds both places on purpose, because a plate needs to know how they sit together: the roof he keeps a listening post on for nine nights, and the alley directly below it where Chet reads Felix out of Pip's memory.

**Everything load-bearing arrived.** Flat bare roof with a low parapet and a brick chimney. A cast-iron drainpipe on wide brackets running the full height of the corner to a hopper at the parapet — the route he climbs. The alley below has the plain steel back door, the dumpster, the pallets, and, the detail this reference exists for, a plastic MILK CRATE and an upturned metal PAINT BUCKET standing about a foot apart. Chet and Pip sit on those two for twenty minutes while Vex watches.

**Nothing the story adds later is present**, which is the `loc-square` rule: no directional dish, no recorder, no cable, no rope and no anchor. Any plate that needs the listening post has to put it there explicitly.

## The brick is RED, and that was a correction rather than a preference

An earlier generation was filed first and then replaced. Its brick measured `rgb(95,72,73)`; `loc-warehouse-ext.png` measures `rgb(90,72,68)`. **A maximum channel difference of five — the same brick.**

That is a real problem rather than a tidy one, because **this arc has two brick back-alleys and they must not read alike.** Chet comes through the wall beside the Warehouse loading step in Part Three, and Chet plugs a cable into the back of Pip's ear in the feed store alley in Part Four. Both alleys have a plain steel door, a dumpster and pallets in them. If the brick matches too, a reader has no way to tell the two scenes apart.

This generation measures `rgb(112,73,51)`, which is plainly a different building, and it also sits better against `loc-main-street.png`, whose frontages run through several colours. Purple stays the Warehouse's alone.

## What was given up, and it is worth knowing

The replaced variant had a drainpipe that visibly continued **over the parapet and onto the roof**, so the climb read in a single picture. This one stops at a hopper head at parapet level, which is what a real drainpipe does and is still obviously climbable, but a plate showing him arriving on the roof has to carry the last few feet itself. Its roof is also a smooth membrane rather than the tarred gravel the prompt asked for.

Five earlier variants are kept for comparison under `patch-notes/incoming/variants-feed-store/`. `feed-store-v5-purple-brick.png` is the one this replaced.
