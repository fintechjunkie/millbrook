# Felix's living room

Use `loc-felix-living-room.png` as the required location reference for `{{LOC:FELIX_LIVING_ROOM}}`. Preserve the layout, the palette and the paper construction. Locked 2026-08-07; see section 6 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md`. Two pages.

Where the whole arc starts. He puts the tape in at three in the morning, falls asleep sitting up forty minutes later with it still running, and Chet arrives at twenty to six and stops to watch it.

**The television is OFF and blank in the reference and that is deliberate.** The picture on it — a man in a mask on a wet rooftop saying that the guilty flinch — is the single most important image in the arc and belongs to the plate, not to the room. Any plate that needs it has to put it there explicitly, which is the `loc-square` rule: a reference is authoritative about what is NOT in the room.

The box of VHS tapes bought for two dollars at the church rummage sale is on the carpet beside the stand, and the coffee table already carries the tools and the half-built thing Chet looks at before he leaves.

**It is a lit room at night**, warmer and slightly dimmer than the other references in this batch. Everything stays readable, but light a plate from this one carefully — the standing rule is that an interior at night is still a light picture.
