**This replaces a one-use stand-in that could not do the job.** `vol4-s03.png` is a frontage, and the five new pages all sit on a feature it does not show: the loading step. Vex sits down on it beside Pip and decides to believe her. Pip pulls her own filter out on it and comes apart saying numbers. Chet arrives through the wall beside it.

**Two things this reference gets exactly right and both were the risk.**

The brick is PURPLE. A previous generation of this building's interior came back brown, and the note has been in the roster ever since.

There is a wide, uninterrupted stretch of plain brick beside the step — no window, no downpipe, no meter box, nothing — running from the ground to well above head height. **A four-and-a-half-foot door opens in that wall**, and it needs somewhere to open. Keep it clear in every plate.

The step itself is two courses high, deep enough for two to sit side by side, with a chipped front edge, and the brick propping the back door open is the same brick the prose has been mentioning since arc one.
