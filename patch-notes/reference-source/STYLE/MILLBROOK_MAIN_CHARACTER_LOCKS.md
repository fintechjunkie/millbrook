# MILLBROOK MAIN CHARACTER LOCKS

Version 2.0  
Status: Canonical and locked  
Applies to: Lena Marsh, Vex, Pip, Milo, Owen, and Felix  
Canonical visual references: `MILLBROOK_CANONICAL_GIRLS_LINEUP_v1.png` and `MILLBROOK_CANONICAL_BOYS_LINEUP_v1.png`

---

## 1. Lock Rule

The canonical lineup is the primary visual source for Lena, Vex, and Pip. Future illustrations may change clothing, pose, expression, age-appropriate styling, props, lighting, and scene context, but must preserve each character's core anatomy, facial design, silhouette, hair construction, skin tone, and permanent identifying features.

When a written prompt conflicts with the canonical lineup, the lineup controls unless a story event explicitly requires a temporary change.

Green skin means a character is sick. Red or flushed skin means a character is angry, overheated, or emotionally activated. Neither is a normal base skin tone.

---

## 2. Shared Construction Standard

All three characters use the locked Millbrook paper-theater style:

- sophisticated layered paper-cut diorama and articulated paper-puppet construction
- matte, fibrous cardstock texture
- visible cut edges and layered hair pieces
- soft contact shadows between paper layers
- slightly oversized heads with expressive eyes
- approximately 5 to 5.5 heads tall
- enlarged hands and shoes for readable acting
- flat paper components rather than rounded CGI volume
- consistent facial geometry across scenes
- full-body silhouettes that remain recognizable without relying on clothing color

Do not reinterpret them as anime, glossy 3D characters, clay figures, photorealistic people, or generic children's-book figures.

## 3. Lena Marsh

### Permanent identity anchors

- fair, warm natural skin tone
- orange hair in two large, uneven side ponytails
- layered, spiky paper hair with loose wisps at the crown
- large brown eyes
- freckles across the nose and cheeks
- narrow youthful face with a small chin
- small oval pendant at the center of the chest
- alert, slightly anxious resting expression
- open, earnest posture with a slight forward lean

### Body and silhouette

- medium height within the girls' trio
- slim, practical build
- slightly narrow shoulders
- long lower legs and large lace-up shoes
- twin ponytails create the dominant silhouette
- usually carries weight slightly forward, as though listening or about to ask a question

### Wardrobe language

Clothing may change, but should remain practical, layered, and journalist-like. Favor:

- orange, rust, warm red, ochre, charcoal, faded navy, and cream
- oversized collared shirts, field jackets, rolled sleeves, patched trousers, practical skirts over leggings, or weather-ready layers
- cross-body satchel, camera pouch, notebook pocket, phone, recorder, or small camera
- worn canvas shoes or practical boots

The oval pendant remains unless the story explicitly removes it. A diagonal strap or visible reporting tool is strongly preferred in most scenes.

### Never change

- do not merge the two ponytails into one
- do not make the hair blonde, brown, black, or red-violet
- do not remove the freckles
- do not give her a confident fashion-model posture
- do not make her expression blank or aloof
- do not use green or red as her normal skin color

## 4. Vex

### Permanent identity anchors

- warm brown natural skin tone
- bright mint-green hair in two messy high buns
- loose pointed strands around the buns and face
- freckles across cheeks and nose
- strong dark eyebrows
- large expressive eyes, usually narrowed or evaluating
- compact face with a firm jaw and slightly upturned chin
- guarded, confrontational resting posture

### Body and silhouette

- slightly shorter than Lena
- compact, athletic build
- broader stance and more grounded center of gravity
- arms often crossed, hands on hips, or occupied with a tool
- the two high mint buns are the dominant silhouette
- clothing creates a short, layered, utility-heavy shape

### Wardrobe language

Clothing may change, but should feel scavenged, practical, rebellious, and ready for physical work. Favor:

- charcoal, black, olive, faded green, rust, muted teal, and small red accents
- cropped or fitted leather and utility jackets
- cargo trousers, patched work pants, heavy belts, tool loops, chains, fingerless gloves, layered socks, and rugged boots
- visibly repaired seams, mismatched patches, hardware-store materials, and improvised fasteners

Her wardrobe should never feel delicate, pristine, formal, or luxury-focused.

### Never change

- do not remove the two mint hair buns
- do not make her skin pale or gray
- do not soften her into a timid or dreamy character
- do not give her elegant, polished posture
- do not use green or red as her normal skin color
- do not make the hair pastel blue, white, or dark green

## 5. Pip

### Permanent identity anchors

- fully robotic humanoid girl, not a cyborg
- pale warm-gray synthetic face material
- sandy-blonde, shoulder-length layered hair
- red bandana tied behind the head
- small round aviator goggles resting on top of the head
- purple lips
- circular mechanical port beside the ear
- clearly visible metallic seams along the jaw, neck, shoulders, elbows, forearms, wrists, fingers, hips, knees, and ankles
- small fasteners, panel joins, and articulated mechanical connections visible at normal scene distance
- large expressive eyes combined with unnaturally precise stillness

### Body and silhouette

- tallest of the three girls in the canonical lineup
- slim, straight build with long limbs
- posture more vertical and balanced than a human teenager's
- head turns and hand placement should feel exact rather than loose
- bandana, goggles, shoulder-length hair, and exposed joint seams form the silhouette
- mechanical construction remains visible even when she is wearing layered clothing

### Wardrobe language

Clothing may change, but should feel repaired, reused, technical, and adapted around a mechanical body. Favor:

- warm cream, faded denim, charcoal, rust red, muted blue, and workwear neutrals
- overalls, utility trousers, mechanic shirts, patched denim jackets, aprons, belts, modular pockets, and practical boots
- openings, rolled sleeves, split cuffs, or tailored gaps that allow the seams and joints to remain visible
- hand-drawn notes, tiny diagrams, stitched repairs, and repurposed components

The red bandana and goggles are recurring anchors and should appear in most images. They may be removed only when the scene specifically calls for it, but Pip must still remain recognizable through her face, hair, purple lips, port, seams, and posture.

### Mechanical visibility rule

Pip's seams are not subtle background details. They must be clearly readable at ordinary illustration scale. Never cover every seam with clothing. At minimum, show visible construction at the jaw and neck plus two or more limb joints. In full-body images, show the arm and leg articulation whenever the pose permits.

### Never change

- do not portray Pip as a human girl with a few decorative metal lines
- do not hide all robotic joints beneath clothing
- do not turn her into an armored robot or chrome android
- do not give her glossy plastic skin
- do not remove the purple lips, ear port, or mechanical jaw and neck construction
- do not use green or red as her normal face color

## 6. Relative Lineup

Use the canonical lineup as the scale reference:

1. Vex is the shortest and most compact.
2. Lena is slightly taller and visually lighter in stance.
3. Pip is the tallest, straightest, and most mechanically precise.

Do not equalize their heights or body builds in group scenes.

---

## 7. Continuity Priority Order

When generating any future image, preserve details in this order:

1. face geometry and eye design
2. hair silhouette and permanent head accessories
3. natural skin or synthetic material color
4. body proportions and relative height
5. permanent marks, freckles, ports, and seams
6. posture and personality
7. wardrobe language
8. scene-specific clothing and props

Clothing is flexible. Identity is not.

---

## 8. Mandatory Prompt Block

Include this block whenever Lena, Vex, or Pip appears:

> Use the canonical Millbrook character models from `MILLBROOK_CANONICAL_GIRLS_LINEUP_v1.png` and the locked specifications in `MILLBROOK_MAIN_CHARACTER_LOCKS.md`. Preserve facial geometry, full-body proportions, relative height, hair silhouette, natural skin tone, permanent accessories, and body language exactly. Clothing may change only within each character's wardrobe language. Green skin indicates sickness and red or flushed skin indicates anger; neither is a canonical base skin tone. Pip is fully robotic and her mechanical seams, ear port, articulated joints, and panel connections must remain clearly visible at ordinary viewing size.

---

## 9. Approval Checklist

Reject or regenerate an image when any answer is no:

- Is Lena recognizable without relying on her shirt color?
- Does Lena have two orange ponytails, freckles, brown eyes, and her oval pendant?
- Is Vex recognizable through warm brown skin, mint double buns, freckles, eyebrows, and compact stance?
- Does Vex look scrappy and practical rather than polished?
- Is Pip visibly and unmistakably robotic?
- Are Pip's jaw, neck, and limb seams readable?
- Does Pip retain sandy-blonde hair, red bandana, goggles, purple lips, and ear port?
- Are Vex, Lena, and Pip shown at their correct relative heights?
- Do all three belong to the same layered paper-cut world?
- Are green and red skin used only as temporary emotional or health states?

## 10. Main Boys Canonical Lock

Version 1.0  
Status: Canonical and locked  
Applies to: Milo, Owen, and Felix  
Canonical visual reference: `MILLBROOK_CANONICAL_BOYS_LINEUP_v1.png`

The approved boys lineup is the controlling visual reference for Milo, Owen, and Felix. Future images may change scene-specific clothing, pose, expression, lighting, and props, but must preserve the face design, body proportions, relative height, hair silhouette, natural skin tone, posture, and recurring identity anchors shown in the canonical lineup.

### Milo

Permanent anchors:

- tallest of the three boys
- slim teenage build with long limbs
- natural light-to-medium skin tone
- dark reddish-brown, jaw-length, slightly messy hair
- orange ribbed knit beanie
- round red-lensed glasses
- clean-shaven face
- narrow, controlled expression and guarded posture
- black leather jacket with silver hardware as his default outer layer
- dark fitted clothing and heavy black boots

Milo should remain calm, unreadable, and visually restrained. Do not use green as his normal skin tone. Green indicates sickness only.

### Owen

Permanent anchors:

- slightly shorter than Milo and taller than Felix
- lean, compact teenage build
- completely bald head
- very pale natural skin
- strong dark brows, narrow eyes, and a flat, mildly irritated expression
- minimal, clinical silhouette
- off-white or warm ivory technical clothing with a high collar
- small red throat closure
- charcoal undershirt and dark shoes
- no glasses, goggles, mechanical seams, robotic joints, or bandana

Owen is human. His pale outfit must remain smooth, clean, and fabric-based so he never reads as Pip. Pip is segmented and mechanical; Owen is minimal and clinical.

### Felix

Permanent anchors:

- shortest of the three boys, but clearly a teenager rather than a child
- compact, boyish build with a straight torso and sturdy hands
- dark brown skin
- short, uneven, tightly textured black hair
- large round red-framed glasses with clear lenses
- bright, alert eyes and a persistent amused half-grin
- lively, slightly forward-leaning builder posture
- faded teal mechanic-style work jacket over a dark shirt
- orange suspenders or harness straps
- dark practical work trousers with functional pockets and tool attachments
- red-orange worn sneakers
- compact brown utility backpack as part of his default silhouette
- small tools, wires, clips, or a handheld invention may appear when appropriate

Felix's clothing must be practical, cleanly designed, and lived-in. Do not use patchwork quilting, floral clothing, ponytails, or a mandatory hat. His personality should come through his glasses, expression, posture, backpack, and functional builder equipment.

### Relative height and silhouette

1. Milo is tallest and narrowest.
2. Owen is slightly shorter, straighter, and visually minimal.
3. Felix is shortest and most compact, with the backpack adding a recognizable rear silhouette.

Do not equalize their heights or body builds.

### Mandatory prompt block for the boys

> Use the canonical Millbrook boys models from `MILLBROOK_CANONICAL_BOYS_LINEUP_v1.png` and the locked specifications in `MILLBROOK_MAIN_CHARACTER_LOCKS.md`. Preserve facial geometry, full-body proportions, relative height, hair silhouette, natural skin tone, posture, and recurring identity anchors exactly. Milo retains his orange beanie, red lenses, jaw-length dark hair, black leather silhouette, and calm guarded presence. Owen remains bald, pale, human, minimal, and clinical in warm ivory technical clothing with a red throat closure, never robotic. Felix remains a short Black teenage builder with textured black hair, round red clear-lens glasses, teal workwear, orange straps, dark work trousers, red-orange sneakers, and his compact utility backpack. Clothing may vary only within each character's established wardrobe language.

### Boys approval checklist

Reject or regenerate when any answer is no:

- Is Milo the tallest, with his orange beanie, red lenses, jaw-length dark hair, and black leather silhouette?
- Does Milo have a natural human skin tone rather than green skin?
- Is Owen completely bald, pale, human, minimal, and free of robotic seams or accessories?
- Is Owen's warm ivory outfit visibly distinct from Pip's segmented mechanical body and workwear?
- Is Felix clearly a Black teenage boy rather than a child or feminine figure?
- Does Felix retain short textured hair, round red-framed glasses, teal workwear, orange straps, functional tools, and his backpack?
- Is Felix's clothing clean utility workwear rather than patchwork?
- Are the three boys shown at the correct relative heights and builds?
- Do they belong to the same layered paper-cut world as the approved girls lineup?
