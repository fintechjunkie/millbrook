# MILLBROOK ANIMAL CHARACTER LOCKS

Version 1.0

These designs are canonical for all future Millbrook illustrations. Clothing and props may vary only where noted. The animal silhouettes, proportions, facial construction, colors, and personality cues should remain consistent.

## Global rendering rules

- Use the locked Millbrook layered paper-cut style.
- Construct each animal from visibly separate flat matte paper pieces.
- Keep cut edges readable and shadows soft and shallow.
- Avoid rounded papier-mache volume, glossy rendering, or sculpted toy surfaces.
- Preserve clean, recognizable silhouettes at small size.
- Do not add default props unless they are specifically listed below.

## Monke

Canonical traits:

- Compact small monkey with a dark charcoal-black body.
- Oversized white circular eyes with tiny dark pupils.
- Long tan muzzle with small nostrils.
- Large round tan inner ears.
- Short messy black head tuft.
- Long curled tail.
- Long narrow hands and feet with tan fingers and toes.
- Odd, watchful, whimsical expression.
- Slightly stiff, deliberate seated or crouched posture.
- Intelligent presence without human clothing or overtly human gestures.

Forbidden drift:

- No permanent granola bar or other default prop.
- No stern gorilla-like brow.
- No realistic primate anatomy.
- No plush-toy cuteness.
- No clothing unless a specific scene explicitly calls for it.

Scene-specific note:

A granola bar may appear only when the story scene specifically includes Monke eating one.

## Warehouse Pig

Canonical traits:

- Small-to-medium pink house pig.
- Round body, short legs, broad snout, floppy ears, curled tail.
- Wide, friendly eyes.
- Calm, comfortable, slightly surprised expression.
- Dark brown hooves.
- Ordinary and domestic rather than magical.

Forbidden drift:

- No farm-hog scale.
- No clothing or accessories by default.
- No exaggerated mud, tusks, or aggressive posture.

## Black Cat

Canonical traits:

- Plump black cat.
- Dense black layered-paper body with subtle separation between head, torso, paws, and tail.
- Pink inner ears and small pink nose.
- Long pale whiskers.
- Usually relaxed, sleepy, or unimpressed.
- Often shown curled on a short stack of books when associated with Hollow Pine Books.

Forbidden drift:

- Do not change the cat to ginger.
- No collars or clothing by default.
- No hyperactive kitten proportions.

## Dino

Canonical traits:

- Small orange cartoon dinosaur.
- Upright stance with a large head, short arms, thick tail, and rounded belly.
- Cream underside.
- Darker orange triangular back spikes.
- Large white eyes and a broad toothy grin.
- Friendly, goofy, energetic personality.

Forbidden drift:

- No realistic dinosaur anatomy.
- No threatening posture.
- No armor, saddle, or clothing by default.

## Croc

Canonical traits:

- Small green gecko-like companion.
- Long low body and tapered tail.
- Wide adhesive toes.
- Large white eyes with uneven, curious focus.
- Light cream underside.
- Muted orange spots along back and tail.
- Alert, comic, inquisitive expression.

Forbidden drift:

- Do not make Croc look like a crocodile.
- No upright bipedal stance.
- No clothing or accessories by default.
