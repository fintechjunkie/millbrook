# Millbrook Secondary Character Locks

Version 1.0 — 2026-07-28

This file defines the canonical secondary male character designs for the Millbrook image system. The approved visual reference is `MILLBROOK_CANONICAL_SECONDARY_MALE_LINEUP_v1.png`.

All future images must preserve facial geometry, body proportions, natural skin tone, age, posture, silhouette, and recurring identity anchors. Clothing may vary only within each character's established wardrobe language.

## Mayor Dalton

Permanent anchors:

- male, in his fifties
- short, compact, pear-shaped build
- thinning uneven dark hair around a balding crown
- large rectangular red-and-blue 3D glasses
- broad ingratiating salesman's grin
- slightly sweaty, overeager public-facing energy
- badly coordinated secondhand-looking suit
- short jacket, slightly long trousers, off-center tie
- muted purple is his default suit color
- cream shirt, dull red patterned tie, mismatched pocket square
- black-and-cream dress shoes
- posture that leans forward as if always selling an explanation

Mayor Dalton should look comic but still belong in the same world as the main cast. Do not turn him into a polished politician, broad slapstick caricature, or realistic adult illustration.

## Admiral Crane

Permanent anchors:

- male, in his sixties
- tall, narrow, upright build
- short gray hair, slightly disordered rather than immaculate
- calm, tired eyes
- pale blue medical mask covering nose and mouth
- simplified dark navy dress uniform
- flat paper medals, restrained gold rank bars, dark tie
- black worn service shoes
- hands held behind his back whenever the scene allows
- controlled, quiet, exhausted authority

Admiral Crane's uniform must remain stylized and paper-built rather than photorealistic military dress. Keep the medal block simplified and the silhouette clean.

## Mr. Henderson

Permanent anchors:

- male, eighty-one years old
- lean elderly build with a slight stoop
- tan, deeply lined face
- tousled white hair
- kind eyes and small genuine smile
- soft faded blue button-down shirt with rolled sleeves
- worn khaki trousers
- brown walking shoes or simple sneakers
- old wristwatch
- relaxed, capable posture rather than frailty
- former shop teacher energy: observant, practical, warm

Mr. Henderson should feel ordinary, lived-in, and specific. Do not render him as a generic storybook grandfather or make him overly frail.

## Relative silhouette

1. Mayor Dalton is shortest and widest.
2. Admiral Crane is tallest and narrowest.
3. Mr. Henderson is between them in height, lean, and slightly stooped.

Do not equalize their heights, builds, or posture.

## Canonical Secondary Female Lineup

Approved visual reference: `MILLBROOK_CANONICAL_SECONDARY_FEMALE_LINEUP_v1.png`

### Cassie

Permanent anchors:

- female, fifteen years old
- small teenage build
- warm natural skin tone
- short brown hair tucked beneath a muted teal knit cap
- hearing aid behind the right ear
- large, observant eyes and a quiet, uncertain expression
- teal long-sleeve shirt
- dark blue denim overalls with rolled cuffs
- black high-top sneakers with cream soles
- slightly closed, cautious posture
- no default backpack, pins, or decorative props

Cassie must remain closely tied to the approved source-character tile: youthful, compact, understated, and unmistakably from the same graphic character family as the main cast. Her hat and overalls are part of her default canonical silhouette. Clothing can vary only when the story requires it, but the teal-and-denim wardrobe language, small build, brown hair, hearing aid, and reserved body language must remain recognizable.

### Aunt Carol

Permanent anchors:

- female adult, designed as stylish middle-aged rather than elderly
- tall, confident, fashion-aware silhouette
- silver-white hair swept dramatically to one side and gathered with a red headscarf
- expressive dark brows and alert eyes
- teal structured blouse or jacket with warm brass buttons
- red scarf or neck accent
- high-waisted wide-leg charcoal trousers
- red-brown belt, ankle boots, and crossbody bag
- upright, self-possessed posture
- practical but distinctly stylish Millbrook wardrobe

Aunt Carol should not look domestic, dowdy, or interchangeable with the Woman in the Green Cardigan. Her visual language is sharper, more contemporary, and more individualized. She may use a tablet or glasses in story scenes, but neither is part of her permanent silhouette.

### The Woman in the Green Cardigan

Permanent anchors:

- female, approximately sixty
- slim, composed older build
- dark gray hair with a bold white streak, arranged in a neat low bun
- green reading glasses on a chain
- calm customer-service smile
- dark green cardigan with visible buttons, including one button in a slightly different shade of green
- pale patterned blouse
- brown plaid calf-length skirt
- dark tights and sensible brown shoes
- hands often lightly clasped
- precise, upright posture with a faintly uncanny composure

The Woman in the Green Cardigan must remain visually distinct from Aunt Carol. She is older, narrower, tidier, more restrained, and anchored by the green cardigan, glasses chain, mismatched green button, low bun, and composed bookstore-professional demeanor.

## Secondary Female Relative Silhouette

1. Cassie is shortest and clearly teenage.
2. Aunt Carol is tallest and most fashion-forward.
3. The Woman in the Green Cardigan is older, slimmer, and more formally composed than Aunt Carol.

Do not equalize their heights, age, posture, hair, or wardrobe language.
