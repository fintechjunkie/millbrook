# Rosa Delgado

Use `char-rosa-canonical.png` as the required visual reference whenever this named figure appears. Preserve the exact face, silhouette, proportions, coloration, and paper construction. Clothing may change only where the character lock permits it.

Rosa Delgado, who parks the taco truck at the little league field on Tuesdays. Locked 2026-08-07; see section 4.8 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md`. Six pages across The Nightjar, more than any other named adult in the first three arcs.

The sheet is neutral on purpose. The morning she closes the truck she is sitting on her own bumper with her keys in her hand and the CLOSED sign written and not taped up, and that is stillness rather than tears. The expression belongs to the plate, the same rule that kept Ida's wave out of hers.

Two recorded deviations from the roster block as written, both kept. The shirt is long-sleeved and pushed to the elbow rather than short-sleeved; the block asked for both at once and the art resolved it sensibly. And she wears brown lace-up work boots, which the block did not specify at all.
