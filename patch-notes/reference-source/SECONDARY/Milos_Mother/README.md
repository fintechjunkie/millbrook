Locked 2026-08-08; see section 4.8 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md`. One appearance, `n1-s04`. Sheet is 1086x1448, exactly 3:4, RGB with no alpha, ground `rgb(237,218,193)`. The author's supplied source art is archived beside this file as `SUPPLIED_SOURCE.png`.

**She exists because the first attempt gave her the wrong face.** `n1-s04` was delivered with her as a Black woman in a headscarf, which came out of the prompt rather than the story and contradicted her son: Milo's block is *natural light-to-medium skin of ordinary healthy tone* with *dark reddish-brown hair*. Everything about this sheet is built to make Milo make sense.

**What arrived and is right:** fair skin with a warm pink flush, a clear scatter of freckles over the nose and cheeks, strong dark brows, heavy-lidded dark eyes with the lids low and level, black hair short at the sides with a dark fringe and jaw-length wisps at the nape, and a completely neutral expression facing the camera. The flowered hat, the dark red roll-neck and the open black blazer are `{{WARDROBE:MOTHER_A}}`.

## One recorded deviation: she reads younger than the block asked for

The block said early forties. The delivered sheet reads closer to **late thirties**: the ageing that was asked for is partly there — faint lines under the eyes and at the nasolabial folds, a fuller jaw than the source — but **the few silver hairs at one temple are absent entirely**.

Kept, and the block has been amended to match the art rather than the other way round, which is the standing rule here. She is unambiguously an adult, which was the point, and at plate distance her age is carried by context more than by line work.

**If she ever needs to read plainly older, silver at the temple is the single most legible cue at plate size** and it is the one thing missing. That is a one-line change to a regeneration, not a redesign.
