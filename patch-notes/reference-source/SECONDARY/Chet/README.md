# Chet

Use `CHET_CANONICAL_FULL_BODY.png` as the required visual reference whenever this named figure appears. Preserve the exact face, silhouette, proportions, coloration, and paper construction.

Chet. Locked 2026-07-31; see section 4.6 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md`.

**He is about four feet tall and the sheet cannot say so.** A figure alone on a flat ground has no scale reference. The height is fixed by the manuscript instead: he steps through a door about four and a half feet tall without stooping. Never draw him man-sized.

**He resembles Pip on purpose.** Pale grey, straw-blond hair, a round port at each side of the head. That family resemblance carries the arc’s last real beat and must survive. They separate on face (he has no mouth and no nose), build, wear and condition.
