# Mr. Jennings

Use `char-jennings-canonical.png` as the required visual reference whenever this named figure appears. Preserve the exact face, silhouette, proportions, coloration, and paper construction. Clothing may change only where the character lock permits it.

Mr. Jennings, the grocer. Locked 2026-08-07; see section 4.8 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md`.

He appeared in two arcs before he had a reference or a token, which is exactly how a character drifts. In The Understudies he puts his hand up at the Mayor's press conference and asks "inspecting what, exactly?"; in The Nightjar he opens the store at two in the morning in his slippers and by half past three is selling club soda and telling people it is the same thing as ginger ale. One man, one performance: helpful past the point of usefulness, and the delivered expression is that and nothing else.

The slippers are a Part One detail and belong to the plate. He wears ordinary brown shoes on the sheet.
