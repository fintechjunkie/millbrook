# The Nightjar

Use `char-nightjar-canonical.png` as the required visual reference whenever the masked figure appears. Preserve the exact face, silhouette, proportions, coloration, and paper construction.

Locked 2026-08-07; see section 4.7 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md` and the wardrobe row `{{WARDROBE:FELIX_B}}`.

**This is Felix's body and that is not negotiable.** In Part Four Chet takes four seconds and Felix opens his eyes in the same gravel in the same cape. This sheet was generated with `char-felix-canonical.png` attached as the authority for face, skin, build, hands and hair, and any regeneration must be too.

**Four things every plate has to hold.** The lenses are flat opaque white with no pupil, no iris and nothing visible through them at any angle. The lower face is bare and fully readable. There are no glasses anywhere, on him or near him. The costume is handmade and slightly wrong.

**He is never unmasked.** Pip asks in Part Two and he refuses: "The Nightjar's face is not the Nightjar's to give away."

**Pip also wears goggles** — hers clear-lensed and pushed up on her forehead over the red bandana, never over the eyes. His are round, black-rimmed, opaque and always over the eyes. They share a great many frames.

## Deviations from the block as written, and what was decided about each

**The coat came back TEAL on the outside** rather than charcoal with a teal lining, and it is kept. The block asked for the teal to be a hidden accent that shows only at a turned lapel; the art made it the garment. Kept because it is the stronger reading: a reader of the first two arcs knows that jacket on sight, so the dramatic irony runs from his first appearance instead of waiting for Part Four, and it costs nothing in-world because nobody in Millbrook can see him anyway.

**He kept Felix's red-orange sneakers** instead of the black work boots the block asked for. Kept, and for the same reason: he never changed his shoes.

**The goggles are round rather than oval.** The supplied reference had wide horizontal ovals; these are circles. Kept, and it is an improvement — Felix's own glasses are large and round, so the disguise carries his eyewear silhouette without carrying his glasses.

**Three small misses, all kept.** The eyebrows are visible above the rims where the block asked for them covered, which helps expression. A little hair shows at the temples under the cap where the block asked for none at the front. And the goggles have no visible head strap.
