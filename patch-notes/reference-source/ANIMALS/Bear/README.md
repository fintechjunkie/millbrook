# The bear

Use `char-bear-canonical.png` as the required visual reference whenever this animal appears. Preserve the exact silhouette, proportions, coloration, and paper construction.

The bear that Millbrook produces on the Thursday after somebody puts up a BEAR CROSSING sign, because the town has to match the document. Locked 2026-08-07; see section 4.8 of `PATCH_NOTES_FLIPBOOK_ROSTER.md`. It is the last image of the arc.

**It is not one of the Warehouse animals and the difference has to stay visible.** The pig, the duck and the dragon are drawn as characters with faces that act. This one is an ordinary animal rendered with real weight: four feet on the ground, no human expression, wearing nothing, holding nothing, never on two legs and never cute. Everything funny about the final page is carried by the people in it, and above all by Mayor Dalton walking toward it with his phone out and his arm already up for the photograph.

One recorded deviation, kept: the animal sits in the upper two thirds of the frame rather than centred, and carries a soft cast shadow the prompt's background line asked not to have. Neither affects its use as a reference.
