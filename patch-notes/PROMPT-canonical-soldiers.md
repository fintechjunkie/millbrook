## One sheet, then carved into four

A group sheet is right for these four and was wrong for the four out of the trunk, for
opposite reasons. Those are four different costumes out of one cupboard, and a shared sheet
homogenises faces — which is how the first knight came back wearing Vex's hair. **These four
are one unit in one uniform, so the risk runs the other way:** generated separately they
drift, and four soldiers who do not match each other are a worse failure than four who are
slightly small in frame. It is the same argument the Warehouse master reference won.

So: generate at 3:1 and **at the largest resolution available**. Four 3:4 panels side by
side is exactly 3:1, which means the carve is four equal quarters with no arithmetic. I cut
it, check each crop decodes clean, and file them as:

## What the prose already fixes

Almost nothing, which is why these four are cheap to cast. Not one of them has a physical
description anywhere in the manuscript. The only constraints are:

- **Dunphy is male and young** — *"he didn't mind it, because people kept bringing him
  things"*, and Rocco drops him with one light exact punch to the jaw.
- **Tovar is male** — *"Tovar put his shoulder into it and it didn't rock."*
- **Reyes is a woman** — Marisol, a sergeant, who hand-carries nine notes across a softball
  field because the only other option is shouting.
- **Pace's gender is never stated**, so she is a free choice and is a woman here.

So **no prose has to change**. The hair colours can be mentioned later if you want them
named on the page, but nothing currently contradicts them.

## Casting, and it is easy to swap

- **Tovar** gets the bearded one because he is the one who physically lays hands on Ottie
  and moves her exactly nothing, so he should look like the strongest of the four.
- **Reyes** gets the cap and the half-smile because she is the one carrying things across a
  field all afternoon without complaining. The stack of notes replaces the wheeled suitcase
  from your sheet, which was carrying a legible label the negative block forbids.
- **Dunphy** gets the green hair because he is nineteen, mild and by the book, and the
  glasses make him the least threatening figure on the page — which is the joke of a
  checkpoint where people keep bringing him doughnuts.
- **Pace** gets the blue hair because she is the one whose orders keep coming out of the
  wrong speaker, and she should look like she knows it.

## Three things excluded from your sheets, deliberately

- **The pig.** It is a locked Millbrook character and cannot be in an Army reference.
- **The wheeled suitcase**, which carried a legible label. The negative block forbids text
  in an image, and a canonical reference is the last place to smuggle one in.
- **Two of the four rifles.** You asked for not-all-guns: Reyes carries notes, Pace has her
  arms folded, and Dunphy's is slung on his back and not in his hands. Only Tovar holds one.
