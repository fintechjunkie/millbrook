## What it is for

`{{LOC:OLD_SCHOOL_C}}` is the third state of a location the series has drawn twice before, and
it carries two pages. `OLD_SCHOOL` is the building abandoned after the Great Event;
`OLD_SCHOOL_B` is the same six acres occupied as a military camp through The Understudies. This
is what it looks like after the unit pulled out in May.

**The emptiness is the picture.** The fence they built is still standing, taut and nine feet
tall with its green slats, and everything they put inside it is gone. The flattened rectangles
in the grass where the tents stood are the only evidence left that any of the previous arc
happened, and they are the reason this image exists rather than a re-use of `OLD_SCHOOL`.

The Nightjar points at this building from a water tower catwalk and says something has been
under it for six months and nobody has ever made it explain itself. Later a directional dish on
the feed store roof is aimed at it. Neither of those is in this reference.
