## Why she exists

`n1-s04` was delivered with her as a Black woman in a headscarf. That came out of my prompt, not
out of the story, and it contradicts her son: Milo is *natural light-to-medium skin of ordinary
healthy tone* with *dark reddish-brown hair*. She now has a block of her own built to make him
make sense — pale skin, dark brows, and black hair that reads reddish in warm light.

## The two things this prompt has to fight

**She has to be aged up and generators do not want to.** The supplied art reads as early
twenties; she is the mother of an eighteen-year-old. Same face, carried into the early forties,
with lines at the eyes and mouth and a little silver at one temple. Everything that makes her
her — the freckles, the heavy lids, the black hair, the pink flush — stays.

**The expression on the source is not the expression on the sheet.** The source is weary and
unimpressed with her head turned away. A canonical sheet is neutral and doing nothing, the same
rule that kept Ida's wave out of hers. Her warmth belongs in the plate, where she is mid-anecdote
about a wedding in 1996 while her son sits on the bathroom floor.
