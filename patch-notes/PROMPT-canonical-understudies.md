# Canonical references: The Understudies, the four out of the trunk

Generated 2026-07-31 from `roster.json`, so STYLE and NEGATIVE below are the current
blocks verbatim rather than a copy that can go stale. Section 4.3 of the roster holds the
immutable descriptions these were built from.

## Generate them ONE AT A TIME, not as a four-up sheet

Asked which is easier: individually, and it is also the only one that is consistent.

- The fourteen existing character references are each a single figure at 900x1200. Every
  spread prompt attaches one of them by filename, so a group sheet would not drop into the
  workflow that already exists.
- A four-up sheet gives each character a quarter of the pixels. These are the images that
  hold a face across dozens of generations, so resolution is the whole point of them.
- A group sheet invites the generator to homogenise four faces toward each other. That is
  not hypothetical here: the first knight came back wearing Vex's hair.

## Two numbers taken from the delivered sheets, not from the old prompt sheet

- **Aspect 3:4, at 900x1200.** `prompt-sheet-canonical.md` says 3:2 in its own header, and
  every one of the fourteen delivered files is 900x1200. For a full figure, head to feet,
  3:4 is right. The header is wrong and is worth correcting separately.
- **Ground `rgb(238, 221, 197)`, stated explicitly.** The old prompt asked for "warm
  off-white paper" and the delivered main-cast sheets came back this flat sand. The exact
  value matters: CLAUDE.md forbids colour-keying a background out of a character plate, so
  a portrait on a panel works by setting the panel to `--portrait-ground` and letting the
  two agree. Cassie came back on 226,199,163 and Crane on 235,212,182, which is the drift
  this line prevents.

After each is approved: save to `public/images/`, drop a copy at
`patch-notes/reference-source/SECONDARY/<Name>/<NAME>_CANONICAL_FULL_BODY.png`, and record
the seed in roster section 4.3.
