# Canonical locations: the rooftop network

Generated 2026-08-07 from `roster.json`, so STYLE and NEGATIVE are the current blocks verbatim
rather than a copy that can go stale. **3:2 and the largest resolution available.**

Built the way the gym, the wardrobe room and Old Bell Road were: empty space, nobody in it, wide
enough to hold the whole of it in one view, generated in the state the prose later changes, with
a MUST HOLD on whatever geometry a plate could contradict.

Drop finished files into `patch-notes/incoming/nightjar-locations/`.

These four are ONE system and they have to agree with each other, which is why they are in one
file. The Nightjar travels Millbrook across its roofs, and three separate references that each
invented their own town would make that impossible to draw.

## The two measurements that are load-bearing

**The tower and the feed store.** *“He'd run a line from the tower's catwalk down to the roof of
the feed store, which is about two hundred feet of cable and a forty-foot drop in the middle of
it.”* He gets ninety feet along it, the far anchor walks, and he ends up hanging **over the middle
of the street** while Vex walks the cable in hand over hand from the catwalk. So: two hundred feet
apart, forty feet of fall, and the line crosses Main Street. Both references show the gap and
neither shows anything strung across it.

**Carter's parapet has to be standable and its back has to be survivable.** He addresses the whole
street from the parapet of a three-storey building, then leaves *“off the back of the roof in a way
that involved a rope and a bicycle frame, and the sound he made landing was not a good sound”*, and
eleven seconds later there is a thumbs up over the parapet. So the parapet is solid and wide, and
there is a rear elevation with a fire escape and an alley to land badly in.

## Everything the story adds later is deliberately absent

The street is generated whole: no hole, no cones, no fresh asphalt, no BEAR CROSSING sign. Part
Three opens a bathtub-sized crater in it and Part Four has the block rebuilt curb to curb with the
storm grate reset level. Both are plate-level changes to this reference.

The feed store roof is bare: no directional dish, no recorder, no cable, no anchor. He builds a
listening post up there over nine nights and none of it belongs in the room he built it in.

The school is generated EMPTY. The unit left in May, and the flattened rectangles in the grass
where the tents stood are the whole point of the picture.
