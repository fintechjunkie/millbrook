## What it is for, and what has already been decided

`{{LOC:WATER_TOWER}}` carries two pages, and they are the two that need it most: the Nightjar
rigs a zip line off the catwalk and gets stuck forty feet up, and Vex goes up the maintenance
ladder without slowing down and walks the cable in hand over hand until she has him. **So the
ladder and the catwalk are the whole point of the picture**, and everything else about the
tower is scenery.

**The tower already has a home and this image must not move it.** `loc-main-street.png` and
`loc-carters-roof.png` both put it beyond the treeline at the end of the street, and the prose
was changed on 2026-08-07 to match them: the line now runs *“from the tower’s catwalk, over the
trees at the end of the road and then down the whole length of Main Street to the roof of the
feed store, which is about four hundred feet of cable and a forty-foot drop in the middle of
it.”* So this reference shows the tower **just beyond a line of trees with the town’s rooftops
past them**, which is where the other two references say it stands.

**Nothing is strung from it.** The cable belongs to the plate, not the room — the same rule
that keeps the directional dish off the feed store roof and the picture off Felix’s television.
