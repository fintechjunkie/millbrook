## TWO SEPARATE IMAGES, IN TWO SEPARATE FILES

These are two unrelated places and they must be generated as **two independent images, saved as
two separate files**. Do not combine them into one picture, do not produce a diptych, a split
frame, a side-by-side comparison, a contact sheet or a grid of options, and do not put both
subjects in a single frame. Run the first prompt, save the result, then run the second prompt
separately and save that result as its own file.

## The style requirement is at the TOP of both prompts, on purpose

Two of the last eleven generations came back photographic with every element of the brief
correct — the drive-in, and a first attempt at this exact feed store. Both times the clause
forbidding photographic realism was sitting in the negative block at the FOOT of the prompt.
Hoisting it to the head fixed the drive-in on one pass, so it is at the head of both prompts
here and it names the specific way each failed.

**Check the style on arrival.** The cheapest check is to open a delivered reference beside the
new one — `loc-old-bell-road.png` is a good yardstick: cut-paper cloud with visible edges,
paper texture in the asphalt.

## What each one is for

**The feed store** carries five pages. Its roof is where the Nightjar keeps a listening post for
nine nights and where the zip line from the water tower is anchored; its alley is where Chet
plugs a cable into the back of Pip's ear and reads Felix out of her memory for twenty minutes.
The milk crate and the paint bucket are what the two of them sit on, so both have to be there.

**The old school** carries two pages and is the only place in this arc where the previous arc is
visible. The Army left in May. The fence they built is still standing and everything they put
inside it is gone, and the flattened rectangles in the grass are the whole picture.

**Both are generated with everything the story adds later absent**, which is the `loc-square`
rule: a reference is authoritative about what is NOT in the room, so a plate that needs the
directional dish or the recorder has to put them back explicitly.
