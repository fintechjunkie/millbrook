# Canonical locations: the six places

Generated 2026-08-07 from `roster.json`, so STYLE and NEGATIVE are the current blocks verbatim
rather than a copy that can go stale. **3:2 and the largest resolution available.**

Built the way the gym, the wardrobe room and Old Bell Road were: empty space, nobody in it, wide
enough to hold the whole of it in one view, generated in the state the prose later changes, with
a MUST HOLD on whatever geometry a plate could contradict.

Drop finished files into `patch-notes/incoming/nightjar-locations/`.

Six independent generations, in any order. Two of them carry a rule that has cost this project
real time before.

**Plates are LIGHT unless the scene is outdoors at night, and an interior at night is still a
light picture.** Felix's living room, the projection booth and the back of the Warehouse are all
night scenes and all three are lit rooms: somebody switched the lights on. Do not write or accept
*“one lamp is the only light source”* for any of them. That instruction was used once on `u2-s09`
and the author had to relight the delivered plate by hand.

**A location reference is authoritative about what is NOT in the room.** `loc-square.png` came back
as an empty civic square, and a prompt that attached it and said nothing produced a press
conference with empty chairs and nobody at the podium. So each of these is deliberately generated
with its later contents ABSENT and the prompt says so by name — no list on the garage wall, no
picture on the television, no cars in the drive-in. Every plate that needs those has to put them
back explicitly.

## Two of these are doing narrative work, not just holding a scene

**The garage is the only physical proof Felix exists.** Monke walks Vex and Pip nine blocks to it
precisely so they will see that somebody lives there — the coffee still in the cup, the bicycles,
the screws sorted by size. The reference needs a clear stretch of blank wall for the taped-up
dragon list and a clear stretch of empty bench for the red glasses, because both are found there
and neither may already be in the picture.

**The loading step behind the Warehouse carries five pages and the current stand-in is a
frontage.** It is where Vex sits down beside Pip and decides to believe her, where Pip pulls her
own filter and comes apart, and where Chet arrives through the brick. It needs a real step wide
enough for two, and plain uninterrupted brick beside it for a four-and-a-half-foot door to open in.
