# Canonical locations: the two Warehouse zones

Generated 2026-08-01 from `roster.json`, so STYLE and NEGATIVE are the current blocks
verbatim rather than a copy that can go stale. **3:2 and the largest resolution available.**

Built the same way as the gym and the wardrobe room: empty space, nobody in it, wide enough
to hold the whole of it in one view, generated in the state the prose later changes, with a
MUST HOLD on whatever geometry a plate could contradict.

**Attach `public/images/loc-warehouse-canonical.png` to BOTH of these.** Section 6.1 of the
roster is explicit about the method: the master is generated first as one wide view of the
whole interior, and the zone references are generated **from** it as nearer views of the same
space. These are not new rooms. They are two ends of one room that already exists.

**Why now, after arc one shipped happily on the stand-in.** The Warehouse carries **five of
Part One’s twelve plates** — spreads 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 — plus nine more mentions across
Volumes 2 to 4. A stand-in was proportionate when it backed a handful of plates. It is not
proportionate when nearly half a volume happens in one room, because every one of those five
plates will be re-deriving the room from a wide master and they will drift apart from each
other rather than from the master.

**Neither may contradict the master.** Same brick, same trusses, same clerestory windows, same
floor. If the master and this prompt ever disagree, the master wins and this prompt is wrong.

### Two fixed features the roster block currently omits, and both are load-bearing

**The refrigerator.** The dragon sleeps on top of it with its tail over the door handle, and
Vex *“pulled her sleeve down over her hand and popped the fridge open with her elbow, because
the dragon was still on the handle”* — which is the setup for the best line she gets in the
volume. The fridge needs a **flat clear top** and a **plain horizontal handle** for any of
that to be drawable.

**The milk crate stool.** Pip sits on it for forty minutes without moving in Volume 1, and
again when Vex crouches in front of her. It is where she is.

Neither appears in the section 6 block for `WAREHOUSE_PIT`. Both are in this prompt, and the
roster block should be amended to match once the reference is approved.

### The empty corner is deliberate

Section 6.1 requires the master to leave one corner near the back wall free, because in
Volume 4 they clear it and put a small desk there for Lena. **This reference inherits that**:
the corner is empty and visibly available. A later plate fills it.

### The high shelf must have a clear run

Monke sits on it and *“watches everything”* in three separate spreads. It needs to be long,
open and sittable, not stacked with boxes.

### The router is the addition

The dragon sleeps on it in Volume 2 and sits up on it in Volume 3, ten feet from Monke on the
high shelf — the two animals noticing the same thing at the same moment, which is the beat.
So the router needs a **flat clear top** at a height something can sit on, and it has to be
placeable ten feet from the shelf. It is not in the current roster block; amend once approved.

### Do not confuse it with the abandoned one

The master carries *“a dark stretch near the back wall with an abandoned second-router
attempt”* — Volume 3: *“where Felix had once been trying to set up a second router and had
given up.”* **That is a different thing in a different place**, it is dead, and it belongs to
the PIT end. The router in this reference is the working one.

### Owen’s desk faces the wall

In Volume 1 he *“didn’t turn around”* and later *“spun his chair around, which he only did
when he had something good.”* The desk is against the brick and the chair turns away from it,
which is what makes the spin readable as an event.
