# Canonical locations: the Warehouse, rebuilt on the delivered plate

Generated 2026-08-01 from `roster.json`, so STYLE and NEGATIVE are the current blocks
verbatim. **3:2, largest resolution available. Generate in the order below** — each one
attaches the one before it.

## The master and the delivered plate disagree, and the plate wins

`loc-warehouse-canonical.png` and `vol3-s07.png` are not the same room. The master puts
Felix's workbench and its pegboard across on the FAR RIGHT and the back door on the FAR LEFT.
The plate puts the bench beside Owen's desk on the left and fills the right wall with
bookshelves and a third couch. Both cannot be true.

**The plate wins, on three grounds.**

1. **No reader has ever seen the master.** It is an internal document. The plate is on a page
   in Volume 3 and has been read.
2. **This project has already ruled this way three times.** Section 1 of the lock file:
   *"When a written prompt conflicts with the canonical lineup, the lineup controls."* Owen's
   eyebrows, all three girls' hair, Monke's colour — every time, the delivered artwork was
   right and the roster text was wrong.
3. **The plate is the better match for the prose.** Felix and Owen are constantly adjacent —
   *"Felix leaned over the back of the chair"* — and the plate seats them a step apart. The
   master has them at opposite ends of the building.

It is also simply a better room: rugs, lamps, bookshelves, warmth. It reads as somewhere
people live rather than a workshop with couches in it.

## The door goes on the right, and the delivered plate is not touched

**Nothing needs to change in `vol3-s07`, and nothing should.** Two reasons.

**First, no delivered plate has ever shown that door.** It exists only in prose — *"the back
door, which Felix had jammed open with a brick again"* — and the one Volume 2 plate that
mentions it only shoots *from* the back-door side. So the door has never been placed on any
page and moving it costs nothing.

**Second, and more important: do not put a shut door where Pip is standing.** It is a good
instinct and it would be a real mistake, because of how The Understudies ends:

> *"Chet stepped through and pulled the door shut behind him, and the wall was a wall again.
> Pip stood in front of it for a long time with her hands flat against her sides."*

**Pip standing perfectly still facing a shut door IS the last image of arc two.** If arc one
already shows her doing exactly that against the Warehouse back wall, a reader arriving at the
ending either reads it as a callback to something unrelated, or thinks it is the same door.
That is a false rhyme, and a planted one is very hard to pull back out.

Her facing plain brick is better. It is already slightly eerie, it explains nothing, and it
leaves the real image unspent.

So the door goes in the back wall on the **right-hand side, past the television rack and
before the bookshelves** — clear of where she stands, propped open with its brick, exactly as
the prose has always had it.

## The fridge, and why it has to be in there

It is not decoration. The dragon sleeps on top of it with its tail over the handle, and Vex
*"pulled her sleeve down over her hand and popped the fridge open with her elbow, because the
dragon was still on the handle"* — which is the setup for the best line she gets in Volume 1.
**That needs a flat clear top and a plain horizontal handle**, or none of it is drawable.

It goes against the back wall a little left of the television rack: wall space the plate
already has, nothing displaced, and reachable from the couches.

## What is being retired

The current `loc-warehouse-canonical.png` is replaced rather than kept alongside. Two masters
for one room is the exact problem section 6.1 was written to prevent, and a stale one will be
attached by mistake eventually. Archive it as a superseded version; do not leave it in
`public/images`.

Section 6 of the roster then needs three amendments: the fridge and the milk crate stool added
to `WAREHOUSE_PIT`, the router added to `WAREHOUSE_TERMINALS`, and the shop moved to the desk
end. I will do those once the references are approved.
