flat cap, the charcoal hooded cape, the teal sleeve and the red-orange sneakers — the whole
`FELIX_B` costume. On the page that introduces him, a duplicate of him at street level is the
worst possible thing in frame, and the negative block already forbids duplicated figures. There
are three figures in this picture in total: him on the roof, and two kids.

**It came back at dusk.** `n1-s09` is the very next moment — he drops off the back of the roof
and everyone laughs — and it is filed as bright mid-morning with a pale blue sky. The two sit on
facing spreads. This one has to match it.
